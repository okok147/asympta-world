# Asympta World for iPhone

Build7 candidate: a near-white paper world populated by72 moving aura lights. No characters or scenery. Five ink colors represent exploration, learning, creating, meeting and resting. Agents travel to places, work after arrival, share ideas during actual encounters, restore energy, then choose another activity. Tap a light for a clearly labeled local-simulation snapshot. These are device-local simulated activities, without external messages or transactions.

Pinch, double-tap and44pt +/- controls support0.45–3.5× zoom; Explore resets the world. Five cached textures and one frame clock avoid repeated aura drawing; inactive/covered/reduced-motion states stop simulation. Existing personal favorites/notes/language/Aura/movement remain in the same version1 save. The first five legacy favorites now represent lights.

See [native architecture](../docs/WORLD_RUNTIME_ARCHITECTURE.md#native-iphone-app-independent-of-web-runtime).
