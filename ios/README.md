# Asympta World for iPhone

Build8 candidate: a near-white paper world populated by72 moving aura lights. No characters or scenery. Five ink colors represent exploration, learning, creating, meeting and resting. Agents travel to places, work after arrival, share ideas during actual encounters, restore energy, then choose another activity. The default2.4× camera follows your light (agent0), marked with a pale ring. No bottom menu. Tap your light → Your Space for personal settings/notes; other lights show local-simulation activity snapshots. These are device-local simulated activities, without external messages or transactions.

Pinch, double-tap and44pt +/- controls support0.45–3.5× zoom; the top-corner return control restores your light/follow. Five cached textures and one frame clock avoid repeated aura drawing; inactive/covered/reduced-motion states stop simulation. Existing personal favorites/notes/language/Aura/movement remain in the same version1 save. The first five legacy favorites now represent lights.
