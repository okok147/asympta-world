# Asympta World for iPhone

Build11 candidate: a near-white paper world populated by72 moving aura lights. No characters or scenery. Each light retains its own pale identity color while exploring, learning, creating, meeting and resting. Tap a light for its five abilities, levels and actual local activity counts. Agents travel to places, work after arrival, share ideas during actual encounters, restore energy, then choose another activity. The default2.4× camera follows your light (agent0), marked with a pale ring. No top or bottom menu or visible controls. Tap your light → Your Space for personal settings/notes; other lights show local-simulation activity snapshots. These are device-local simulated activities, without external messages or transactions.

Pinch supports0.45–3.5× zoom, drag explores, and double-tap paper restores2.4× follow on your light. Preloaded identity textures and one frame clock avoid repeated aura drawing; inactive/covered/reduced-motion states stop simulation. Existing personal favorites/notes/language/Aura/movement remain in the same version1 save. The first five legacy favorites now represent lights.
